Is Avon or Hatteras Village better for families?

Both work. Avon is the practical base: it has a full-size grocery store, restaurants and surf shops, and it sits mid-island. Hatteras Village is quieter, at the end of the road, with a working harbor and the Ocracoke ferry.

From Avon, Buxton's lighthouse and Hatteras Village are both a short drive. From Hatteras Village, everything else on the island is north of you.

Families who want errands to be easy tend to choose Avon. Families who want fishing boats and a day on Ocracoke choose Hatteras Village.
